## Android Open Source Project Pinyin dictionary

`app/src/main/res/raw/pinyin_dictionary.tsv` is a modified, UTF-8, frequency-
sorted extract of `jni/data/rawdict_utf16_65105_freq.txt` from the
[Android Open Source Project PinyinIME repository](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/packages/inputmethods/PinyinIME/).
InkTalk groups entries by untoned pinyin
and retains at most 12 high-frequency candidates for each key. Source revision:
`49aebad1c1cfbbcaa9288ffed5161e79e57c3679`.
